# Inbox

Your inbox is where you receive notifications about activity on your Reddit account. It shows replies to your comments, comments on your posts, and comments that mention your username, and gives you quick access to [Chat](hydra://settings/guide/?doc=chat).

## Accessing Your Inbox

Tap the **Inbox** tab in the bottom tab bar to view your inbox. A badge on the tab shows how many unread items you have and updates automatically. The inbox refreshes when you open it, and you can pull down to manually refresh at any time. Older items load automatically as you scroll.

## Replies

Replies appear when someone responds to one of your comments or posts, or mentions your username in a comment. Each reply shows the post title, subreddit, author, reply content, vote count, and how long ago it was sent. Tapping a reply opens it in context within the full discussion.

Unread items are highlighted so you can easily spot what's new.

## Interacting with Inbox Items

### Tapping

Tap any inbox item to open the post or comment in context. Tapping an unread item automatically marks it as read.

### Swiping

- **Swipe right** to upvote (short swipe) or downvote (longer swipe)
- **Swipe left** to mark an item as read

### Long Press

Long pressing a reply gives you options to **Upvote**, **Downvote**, or **Mark as Read** (for unread items). Reddit doesn't support marking items as unread again.

### Mark All Read

Tap the **checkmark icon** in the top-right corner of the inbox to mark all items as read at once.

## Chat

Direct conversations with other users happen in Reddit Chat rather than the inbox. Tap the **chat bubble icon** in the top-right corner of the inbox to open your chats. Learn more in the [Chat guide](hydra://settings/guide/?doc=chat).

## Inbox Alerts (Pro)

With [Hydra Pro](hydra://settings/hydraPro), you can receive push notifications when new items arrive in your inbox so you never miss a reply. Learn more in the [Inbox Alerts guide](hydra://settings/guide/?doc=inbox_alerts).
