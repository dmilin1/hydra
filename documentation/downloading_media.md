# Downloading Media

You can download images and videos to your device's photo library through the share sheet.

## Saving a Video

**Tap** the video to open it full screen, then tap the **share button** in the overlay. Hydra downloads the video first. A progress ring fills around the button so you can track the download. Then it opens the share sheet, where you can tap **"Save Video"**.

Reddit-hosted videos are saved with their audio included if you have [Hydra Pro](hydra://settings/guide/?doc=hydra_pro). Without Pro, when a Reddit-hosted video has audio Hydra asks whether you'd like to download it without audio or get Hydra Pro.

Saving also works for videos from RedGifs, Imgur, and Gfycat. These are saved with their audio included for everyone.

## Saving an Image

**Long press** the image to open the share sheet, then tap **"Save Image"**. You can also tap the image to view it full screen and use the **share button** in the overlay.

## Permissions

On iOS, make sure Hydra has permission to save to your photo library. If prompted, grant access so media can be saved. You can manage this in your device's Settings under Privacy & Security > Photos. On Android, saving is handled by the app you share to, so no photo permission is needed.
